A separate `CTX-001 --token-budget 4000` probe exited 3 with
`RETRIEVAL_INSUFFICIENT`/`PARTIAL`. This matches the architecture's statement
that defaults are measurements rather than promises: on this CTX version and
small corpus, 4,000 tokens is not enough for CTX's serialized completeness
metadata even though Factory's own mandatory material fits. Factory fails closed
instead of presenting the partial result as sufficient.
